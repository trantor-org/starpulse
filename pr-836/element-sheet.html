Phone-width bar

At 900 px and narrower the navigator is hidden and a bar along the bottom edge switches views; its More opens the navigator as a sheet. The bar's rules sit under the stylesheet's max-width: 900px query, so each specimen is a 390 px frame of its own, filled by elements.js with this page's stylesheet. The bar is calc(38px + 16px * var(--fs)) tall: 54 px at 100% text, 62 px at 150%.

Phone-width lane strip

At 900 px and narrower the Kanban draws one lane at a time under its own header, picked on a strip above the lanes. The strip's rules sit under the same query, so each specimen is a 390 px frame filled by elements.js; above 900 px the strip is not drawn and all six lanes sit side by side.
